MUSIC GLOW 1.0.0 • macOS
by Jacob Mobin

Your music, around your Dock.

1. Unzip the download and drag Music Glow.app to Applications.
2. Open Music Glow. Its waveform icon appears in the menu bar.
3. Open Apple Music and play a song on your built-in MacBook display.
4. Allow Music automation and system audio recording when macOS asks.
5. Use the waveform menu to adjust sensitivity, preview, retry, or quit.

REQUIREMENTS
macOS 14.2 or later, Metal, and a built-in display. Downloads are labeled
with their architecture: arm64 is Apple Silicon; x86_64 is Intel.
External and mirrored displays are intentionally excluded.

FIRST LAUNCH
This community build is ad-hoc signed, not Developer ID signed or notarized.
macOS may block a downloaded copy. After checking the download's origin,
use System Settings > Privacy & Security > Open Anyway if offered.
If your Mac's policy prevents this, build from source instead. Do not
disable Gatekeeper or remove quarantine recursively.

PERMISSIONS
Music automation reads the song and album artwork. System audio recording
allows local analysis of Music's audio. No recordings are saved or uploaded.
If you denied access, enable Music Glow in Privacy & Security > Automation
and the audio recording section, relaunch, then choose Retry Music & Audio.
The app never changes playback, your output device, or login items.

NO GLOW?
Enable Glow in the waveform menu and try Visual Preview (30 seconds).
That preview uses generated input. Actual playback requires a capturable
Music audio signal. Protected/unavailable audio may not produce a signal.
Keep your laptop display open and unmirrored. For the intended appearance,
place your Dock along the bottom. The app is silent while Music is paused.

UNINSTALL
Quit Music Glow from its menu and move the app to Trash.
